## Final reconciliation; WU-1299

All 74 inventoried page entry points still exist. Rollout ownership remains in the
Rollout column; the Final outcome column distinguishes migrated and deliberately
excluded surfaces. No route is blocked on new SQL. Baseline columns are retained
for comparison rather than overwritten with the new behavior. Detailed shipped
capabilities and evidence are in [progress](progress.md) and
[verification](verification.md).

All comparable directories now use the shared controls and safe-return contract.
Server-paged Clans and Scrolls retain server paging. Release logs retain GET/server
paging and their original invalid-page behavior. Legacy squad and curated music
pages retain authored narrative/media and gain shared context/navigation. Article
bodies remain unchanged. Diagnostics, administrative apps, analytics and unrelated
editorial pages retain their purpose-built layouts. Shared loading/error/not-found
boundaries remain in use; fixture checks do not imply live service availability.
